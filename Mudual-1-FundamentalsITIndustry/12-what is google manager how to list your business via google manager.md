# What is Google Business Profile (Google Manager)?

Google Business Profile, previously known as Google My Business, is a free tool provided by Google that helps businesses manage their online presence on Google Search and Google Maps. It allows businesses to create and update their business profile so that customers can find them easily.

It is commonly called “Google Manager” by users because it lets business owners manage their listing and business information on Google.

## Why is Google Business Profile Important?

- Helps customers find your business
- Shows your location on Google Maps
- Displays your contact details, hours, and website
- Builds trust and credibility
- Increases visibility in local search results
- Helps attract nearby customers

## Main Features of Google Business Profile

- Business name
- Business address
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Business hours
- Photos and videos
- Reviews and ratings
- Google Maps listing
- Business description

## How to List Your Business via Google Business Profile

Follow these steps to create or update your business listing:

### Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile
Open your browser and visit:
https://www.google.com/business/

### Step 2: Sign in with Google Account
Use a Google account that belongs to your business. If you do not have one, create a new Google account.

### Step 3: Add Your Business
Click on the option to “Add your business to Google.”

Enter the following details:
- Business name
- Business category
- Business location
- Service area (if applicable)
- Contact number
- Website

### Step 4: Choose Business Location
You can choose one of the following:
- Storefront business: has a physical location
- Service-area business: visits customers at their location
- Online business: sells services online without a physical store

### Step 5: Verify Your Business
Google will ask you to verify ownership of the business. Common methods include:
- Postcard by mail
- Phone verification
- Email verification
- Video verification

Once verified, your business listing will be activated.

### Step 6: Complete Your Business Profile
Add important information such as:
- Business description
- Opening hours
- Photos
- Services offered
- FAQs
- Customer reviews

### Step 7: Manage and Update Regularly
You can update your business profile anytime to keep information accurate and current.

## Benefits of Listing Your Business on Google

- People can search for your shop or services nearby
- Your business appears on Google Maps
- Customers can contact you easily
- More leads and sales opportunities
- Builds trust through reviews

## Important Tips

- Use the correct business name
- Add accurate address and phone number
- Use real photos
- Keep business hours updated
- Respond to customer reviews
- Add relevant keywords in your business description

## Summary
Google Business Profile is a free Google tool that helps businesses create and manage their online presence. It allows business owners to list their company on Google Search and Google Maps, making it easy for customers to find them. By verifying and updating the business profile correctly, owners can attract more customers and improve local visibility.

## Short Definition
Google Business Profile = A free Google tool for managing a business listing on Search and Maps.
