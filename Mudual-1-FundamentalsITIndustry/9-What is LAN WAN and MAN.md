# What is LAN, WAN, and MAN?

These are different types of computer networks that connect devices and allow communication over a certain area.

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)
LAN is a network that connects computers and devices within a small area such as a home, office, school, or building.

Features:
- Small area coverage
- High speed
- Low cost
- Usually privately owned

Examples:
- Office computers connected to one network
- Wi-Fi network in a classroom
- Home internet network

Advantages:
- Fast data transfer
- Easy sharing of resources
- Low cost

Disadvantages:
- Limited coverage area
- Security issues if not configured properly

## 2. WAN (Wide Area Network)
WAN is a network that connects computers and networks over a large geographical area such as cities, countries, or even continents.

Features:
- Large area coverage
- Slower than LAN
- Uses public or private communication links
- Usually managed by telecom providers

Examples:
- Internet
- International company networks
- Bank networks across cities

Advantages:
- Connects distant locations
- Supports global communication

Disadvantages:
- More expensive
- More complex to manage
- Lower speed compared to LAN

## 3. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
MAN is a network that covers a larger area than LAN but smaller than WAN. It usually covers a city or a metropolitan area.

Features:
- Covers a city or large campus
- Connects multiple LANs together
- Medium range
- Usually used by organizations, universities, or government bodies

Examples:
- City government network
- University network across campus buildings
- Cable television network

Advantages:
- Connects many local networks
- Good for city-level communication

Disadvantages:
- More costly than LAN
- Requires proper infrastructure

## Comparison of LAN, WAN, and MAN

| Network Type | Coverage Area | Speed | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAN | Small area like home/office | High | Office network |
| MAN | City or metropolitan area | Medium | City network |
| WAN | Large area like country/world | Lower than LAN | Internet |

## Summary
- LAN connects devices in a small area.
- MAN connects devices in a city or metropolitan region.
- WAN connects networks across large distances, including countries and continents.

## Short Definition
- LAN = Local Area Network
- MAN = Metropolitan Area Network
- WAN = Wide Area Network
